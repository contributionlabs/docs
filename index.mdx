Megaphone is the easiest way to launch allowlists, points campaigns, and airdrop claims.

1. **Viral Allowlist**: Before launching your next feature or product, give early access to your top community contributors who help you build your brand the most.
1. **Points Campaign**: Questing pages that target social engagement (X, Discord, Telegram, etc.) and high-value onchain actions (swaps, deposits, liquidity provisioning, etc.), with a beautiful interface that puts your brand first.
1. **Airdrop Eligibility Checker**: Generate hype for your airdrop by allowing users to check if they are eligible to claim, and participate in useful social and onchain actions to earn some allocation.
1. **Token Claim**: Allow your community to claim their tokens and participate in your onchain ecosystem.

We're on a mission to increase the economic success of the best onchain communities. Megaphone is here to help you connect with your users, streamline engagement operations, fight bots, and reward top contributors.
